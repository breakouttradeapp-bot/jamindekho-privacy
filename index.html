Privacy Policy — JaminDekho

Last updated: 24 November 2026

JaminDekho ("the app") is published by an independent developer. It is not an official government application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or acting for any central or state government department. Land records shown in the app come from the official state government websites named in the app. The app has no account system and no backend server.

1. Stored only on your phone

  • App language and theme
  • Your chosen state and weather city
  • Favourite states and recently opened websites (up to 20)
  • Daily farmer-update setting (on/off and time)
  • The last weather and news results, so the app works offline

None of this is sent to the developer. Clear it in Settings, or uninstall the app.

2. Getting a record inside the app

For some states the app fills the official government form for you. The district, village, survey/khata number and the CAPTCHA you type are sent directly from your phone to that state's official website (for example bhulekh.mahabhumi.gov.in, anyror.gujarat.gov.in, landrecords.karnataka.gov.in), exactly as a web browser would.

  • The record the website returns is shown on your phone only.
  • Nothing is sent to the developer and no copy is kept. Each search starts fresh; the website's cookies are cleared first.
  • Save PDF / Save Image create the file on your phone and open Android's share screen. The file goes only where you choose.
  • The app never solves CAPTCHAs or OTPs for you; you type them yourself.

3. Government websites opened in the app

Other records open the official website inside the app's own browser screen (only https sites under gov.in or nic.in).

  • Anything you enter there (district, survey number, mobile number, OTP, CAPTCHA) goes only to that website, under its own privacy policy.
  • The app does not read, change, copy or upload what you enter.
  • Cookies set by those websites stay on your phone so your session keeps working.
  • Files a website offers for download are saved by Android's Download Manager.

4. Weather

To show the forecast, the app sends the approximate centre coordinates of the city you selected (not your phone's location) to Open-Meteo.com. Weather data is licensed CC BY 4.0. The app does not request location permission.

5. News

The app downloads public news feeds from government sources (Press Information Bureau, India Meteorological Department). No personal information is sent with these requests.

6. Notifications

If you turn on the daily farmer update, the app schedules local notifications on your phone. There is no push server; no notification data leaves your device. Turn it off at any time in Settings.

7. Permissions

Permission Why
INTERNET Load government websites, records, weather and news
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Show a clear message when you are offline
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Only if you turn on the daily farmer update
AD_ID Lets Google AdMob show and measure ads (see section 8)

No contacts, SMS, location, camera, microphone or storage permission is requested.

8. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob to keep the app free. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID and basic device information (model, Android version, language),
  • your IP address, from which Google may infer an approximate region,
  • information about how you interact with ads (views and taps).

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by the developer, under Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. The land-record details you type or see in the app are never shared with AdMob.

Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).

To open a land record inside the app, you watch one short rewarded ad first (you are told before it plays and can cancel). Saving a record as PDF / image also needs a short rewarded ad. You can always open the official government website for free without ads.

The app contains no other analytics SDK.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from them.

10. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date above changes and the About screen will mention it.
